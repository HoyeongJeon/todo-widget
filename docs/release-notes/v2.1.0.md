- **Windows: taskbar button.** Click it to bring the widget to the front when other windows cover it. Click it again to send the widget behind them and go back to the window you were using. With the pin on, the widget stays on top. Right-click the button to pin it to the taskbar or close the widget.
- **Windows: Alt+Tab and `Win+M`.** The widget now appears in Alt+Tab, and `Win+M` minimizes it with your other windows. Click the taskbar button or open TodoWidget again to bring it back where it was.

Nothing changes on macOS. Your tasks and settings stay as they are.

## Update

**From v2.0.0:** click **Update** on the line at the bottom of the widget. The widget looks for a new version when it starts and once a day.

## Install

**Windows 10/11 (x64):** Using v1.4? Quit it first (⋯ → 종료) and see [Upgrading from v1.4](https://github.com/HoyeongJeon/todo-widget#upgrading-from-v14-windows). Download `TodoWidget_2.1.0_x64-setup.exe` and run it. It installs for your user only, without administrator rights. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" (SmartScreen), click **More info** → **Run anyway**. The installer is not code-signed.

**macOS 13 or later (Apple Silicon and Intel):** Download `TodoWidget_2.1.0_universal.dmg`, open it, and drag **TodoWidget** into **Applications**. Open it from Applications, not from inside the dmg. macOS blocks the first launch because the app is not notarized. Go to **System Settings → Privacy & Security** and click **Open Anyway**. After that it opens normally.

The `.sig`, `.app.tar.gz`, and `latest.json` files are for automatic updates. You don't need to download them.
